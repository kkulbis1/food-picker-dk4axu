# Cook Day 1 — 2026-10-31 (2 hours hands-on)

**Result: 16 two-person dinners** (about 32 servings), all household favorites or built from approved ingredients. **No oven needed today** — everything is assembled raw or unbaked and frozen; you bake or cook it on the day you eat it.

| # | Dish | Makes | Basis | Cheese |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Chicken & Mushroom Tetrazzini (lighter) | 4 pans × 2 servings | ✏️ household favorite, modified (cheese-free sauce) | none (parmesan optional at serving) |
| 2 | Chicken Enchiladas Verde | 4 pans × 2 servings | ✅ household favorite | 2 pans with cheese, 2 without |
| 3 | Chicken Tater Tot Casserole | 4 pans × 2 servings | ✅ household favorite (as approved — no cheese) | none |
| 4 | Beef, Broccoli & Mushroom — pressure-cooker prep bag | 2 bags × 2 servings | 🆕 new, not-carb-heavy | none |
| 5 | Chicken, Mushroom & Green Bean — sheet-pan prep bag | 2 bags × 2 servings | 🆕 new, not-carb-heavy | none |

### Raw vs. cooked — read before labeling
| Item | State when frozen | On the day you eat it |
|---|---|---|
| Tetrazzini, Enchiladas, Tater Tot (pans) | **Fully cooked chicken + sauce, unbaked assembly** | Bake until **165°F in the center** (steaming) |
| Beef, Broccoli & Mushroom bag | **RAW beef** | Pressure-cook until the beef is cooked through (well past 145°F) |
| Chicken, Mushroom & Green Bean bag | **RAW chicken** | Roast or air-fry until the chicken reaches **165°F** |

- Write **RAW** in large letters on prep bags and freeze them on a sheet pan away from ready-to-eat food.
- Thaw everything **in the fridge**, never on the counter. Thaw raw bags on a plate on the bottom shelf, and cook within 1–2 days of thawing.
- Best quality within about 3 months. Put "use by" dates on the labels.
- If 6½ lb of chicken goes above your pressure cooker's max-fill line, cook it in 2 batches. That adds about 25 unattended minutes.

**Cheese check:** 2 of 16 dinners have cheese (13%) — well under the household 30% limit, leaving room for parmesan at serving.
**Decisions applied:** no alcohol anywhere; Smart Balance / Melt replaces butter; 2-person portions.

## Shared base: shredded chicken
- 6½ lb boneless skinless chicken breasts
- 1½ cups gluten-free chicken broth
- 1 tsp salt

Cook: pressure cooker, high pressure 12 min, natural release 10 min. Confirm **165°F** in the thickest piece, then shred. This makes about 13 cups. Split it: **4 cups** to Tetrazzini, **5 cups** to Enchiladas, **4 cups** to Tater Tot.

## Shared base: big mushroom sauce (for dishes 1 and 3)
- 6 Tbsp Smart Balance or Melt
- 2 onions, diced
- 2½ lb sliced mushrooms
- ⅔ cup gluten-free all-purpose flour
- 5 cups gluten-free chicken broth
- 3 cups 2% milk
- 2 tsp dried thyme
- 1½ tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp garlic powder (mild — household prefers mild garlic)
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper

1. In the large pot over medium-high, melt the butter substitute. Cook the onions and mushrooms about 10 min, until the mushroom liquid has mostly cooked off.
2. Stir in the flour for 1 minute.
3. Slowly whisk in the broth, then the milk.
4. Simmer, stirring, about 5 min until thick enough to coat a spoon. Season.
5. Pour **about 5 cups into a bowl for the Tater Tot Casserole**. Leave the rest in the pot for the Tetrazzini.

## 1. Chicken & Mushroom Tetrazzini (lighter) — 4 two-person pans
Add to the sauce left in the pot:
- ¼ cup nutritional yeast (labeled GF)
- 4 cups shredded chicken
- 12 oz GF spaghetti or penne, cooked 2 min under package time
- 1½ cups frozen peas (no need to thaw)

Mix well and divide among 4 foil pans.
**Label:** *Thaw overnight in the fridge. Bake covered at 350°F for 35–40 min, until 165°F in the center. Parmesan optional at serving. From frozen: about 70 min.*

## 2. Chicken Enchiladas Verde — 4 two-person pans (4 enchiladas each)
- 5 cups shredded chicken
- 2 jars (16 oz each) **mild** salsa verde
- ½ cup sour cream
- 1 cup frozen corn
- 1 tsp cumin
- ½ tsp salt
- 16 corn tortillas (labeled GF)
- 1½ cups shredded Monterey Jack (pasteurized) — **for 2 pans only**

1. Mix the chicken, 1 cup of the salsa, the sour cream, corn, cumin and salt.
2. Warm the tortillas so they roll without cracking: wrap 8 at a time in a damp paper towel and microwave 45 sec.
3. Spread 2 Tbsp salsa in each pan. Fill each tortilla with about ⅓ cup of the mix, roll it, and lay it seam-down, 4 per pan.
4. Top each pan with the remaining salsa (about ½ cup per pan).
5. Put the cheese on **2 pans only**. Label those "CHEESE" and the other 2 "NO CHEESE".

**Label:** *Thaw overnight. Bake covered at 375°F for 25 min, then uncovered for 10 min, until 165°F and the cheese is bubbling. From frozen: covered 50 min, then uncovered 10 min.*

## 3. Chicken Tater Tot Casserole — 4 two-person pans
- 4 cups shredded chicken
- The ~5 cups of mushroom sauce you set aside
- 4 cups frozen mixed vegetables
- 2 lb frozen tater tots (labeled GF)

1. Mix the chicken, sauce and vegetables, and divide among 4 pans.
2. Top with a single layer of frozen tots. Lid, label, freeze.

**Label:** *Bake from frozen at 400°F: covered 30 min, then uncovered 25–35 min until the tots are crisp and the center is 165°F. Or reheat a portion in the air fryer at 375°F until steaming.*

## 4. Beef, Broccoli & Mushroom — pressure-cooker prep bags (2 bags)
**Per bag:**
- 1 lb beef stir-fry strips
- 8 oz sliced mushrooms
- ⅓ cup GF tamari
- 2 Tbsp honey
- 1 Tbsp brown sugar
- 1 Tbsp ginger paste
- ¼ tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- ½ cup GF broth

Label **"RAW BEEF — pressure-cook"** with the date. Squeeze out the air, lay the bag flat and freeze it. Keep frozen broccoli separate — **3 cups per bag on cooking day**.

**Cooking day:**
1. Thaw overnight. Dump the bag into the pressure cooker. High pressure 8 min, quick release.
2. Switch to sauté. Stir in 1 Tbsp cornstarch mixed with 2 Tbsp water.
3. Add the broccoli, put the lid on (no pressure) for 3–4 min until the broccoli is tender.
4. Beef cooked through. Serve as-is or with a small scoop of rice.

## 5. Chicken, Mushroom & Green Bean — sheet-pan prep bags (2 bags)
Label **"RAW CHICKEN — cook to 165°F"** with the date.

**Per bag:**
- 1¼ lb boneless chicken thighs, cut into 1½-inch pieces
- 8 oz mushrooms
- 12 oz green beans, trimmed
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 1½ tsp Italian seasoning
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ¼ tsp garlic powder
- ¾ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp pepper

Squeeze out the air, lay the bag flat and freeze.

**Cooking day:** thaw overnight. Spread on a lined sheet pan and roast at 425°F for 22–25 min, until the chicken reaches **165°F**. Or use the air fryer at 400°F for about 15 min, shaking halfway. Parmesan optional.
