### Gluten cross-contact
This is a GF-only kitchen session. If wheat products are ever used in the house, wash the colander, wooden spoons, food processor and pans before you start. Use only GF-labeled ingredients today.

### Thermometer checkpoints
1. Pressure-cooker chicken: **≥165°F** in the thickest piece. If it's under, seal and pressure-cook 3 more minutes.
2. Oven chicken: **≥165°F** in the thickest piece. If it's under, give it 5 more minutes and recheck.
3. Cooling: pans go in the fridge within **2 hours** of cooking (sooner is better).
4. Before freezing the Tater Tot pans: **≤40°F**.
5. Reheat day: casseroles **165°F in the center**. Beef: **145°F + 3-min rest** (color is not a test). Raw-bag chicken: **165°F**.
6. **Wash and sanitize** the thermometer probe between raw and cooked checks.

### Raw/cooked separation
- Handle raw meat first, on the raw board only, then wash and sanitize before you touch cooked chicken.
- Use a clean fork or tongs to take cooked chicken out. Never use the tongs that handled raw breasts.
- Thaw raw bags in the fridge on a plate on the bottom shelf, and cook within 1–2 days.

## Shared base: shredded chicken
- 6½–7 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts. **Choose "no added solution" / not enhanced** (enhanced chicken has added salt).
- 2 cups unsalted GF chicken broth (1½ cups for the pressure cooker, ½ cup for the oven).
- No added salt.

Expected yield: 12–15 cups shredded (about 25–30% weight loss during cooking). Split it **4 cups** Tetrazzini, **5 cups** Enchiladas, **4 cups** Tater Tot. If you're short, take it from the Enchiladas and add ½ cup more corn.

## Shared base: big mushroom sauce (for dishes 1 and 3)
- **Fat for the roux:** 7 Tbsp Smart Balance/Melt **if you have it at home**, or **5 Tbsp olive oil**. None is in the cart (Kovas: skip). Spreads are about 64% fat versus about 80% for butter, so it takes about 7 Tbsp of spread to match 6 Tbsp of butter. The olive-oil roux thickens the same way, tastes slightly less rich, and has less saturated fat
- 2 onions, diced
- 2½ lb sliced mushrooms
- ⅔ cup GF all-purpose flour
- 5 cups **unsalted** GF chicken broth
- 3 cups 2% milk
- 2 tsp dried thyme
- 1½ tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp garlic powder (mild)
- **¾ tsp salt**
- 1 tsp pepper

Steps:
1. Heat the spread or oil in the Dutch oven over medium-high. Cook the onions and mushrooms **15–20 min** until the liquid has mostly cooked off.
2. Stir in the flour for 1 minute.
3. Slowly whisk in the broth, then the milk.
4. Simmer and stir about 5 min until it coats a spoon. Taste before adding any more salt.
5. Set aside **about 5 cups** for the Tater Tot Casserole. The rest stays in the pan for the Tetrazzini.

## 1. Chicken & Mushroom Tetrazzini (lighter) — 4 two-person pans
Stir into the sauce left in the pan:
- ¼ cup nutritional yeast (labeled GF)
- 4 cups shredded chicken
- 12 oz GF spaghetti or penne, cooked 2–3 min under package time and rinsed cool (~1½ oz dry per serving; a lighter portion)
- 1½ cups frozen peas

Divide among 4 pans, about 4 cups per pan.
**Label:** *TETRAZZINI — cooked, NOT baked. Thaw overnight in the fridge. Bake covered at 350°F 35–40 min to 165°F center (from frozen ~70 min). Parmesan optional. Use by [date + 3 months].*

## 2. Chicken Enchiladas Verde — 4 two-person pans (4 enchiladas each)
- 5 cups shredded chicken
- 2 jars (16 oz) **mild** salsa verde, labeled GF. **About 2½ cups total used**
- ½ cup sour cream
- 1 cup frozen corn
- 1 tsp cumin
- No added salt
- 16 corn tortillas (labeled GF)
- 1½ cups shredded Monterey Jack (pasteurized), **for 2 pans only**

**At 0:40–1:00:** mix the chicken, ½ cup salsa, sour cream, corn and cumin. Cover and refrigerate.
**At 1:50 (or Sunday if you hit the safety valve):**
1. Take the filling out of the fridge.
2. Microwave the tortillas 8 at a time in a damp paper towel for 45 sec.
3. Spread 2 Tbsp salsa in each pan.
4. Fill each tortilla with about ⅓ cup of filling, roll, and place seam-down, 4 per pan.
5. Top each pan with about ⅓ cup salsa. Put cheese on **2 pans only**.

**Label:** *ENCHILADAS VERDE — CHEESE / NO CHEESE — cooked, NOT baked. Thaw overnight. Bake covered at 375°F 25 min + uncovered 10 min to 165°F (from frozen: covered 50 + uncovered 10). Pasteurized cheese can go on before baking.*

## 3. Chicken Tater Tot Casserole — 4 two-person pans
- 4 cups shredded chicken
- The ~5 cups of reserved sauce
- 4 cups frozen mixed vegetables
- 2 lb frozen tater tots (labeled GF). **Add these after the filling is cold.**

Mix the chicken, sauce and vegetables and divide among the pans, then refrigerate. Once cold, top with a single layer of frozen tots (cover about ¾ of the surface for less sodium), close the lids and freeze.
**Label:** *TATER TOT — cooked, NOT baked. Bake from frozen at 400°F covered 30 min, then uncovered 25–35 min until the tots are crisp and the center is 165°F.*

## 4. Beef, Broccoli & Mushroom — RAW pressure-cooker bags (2)
**Per bag:**
- 1 lb beef stir-fry strips (plain, unseasoned)
- 8 oz sliced mushrooms
- **2 Tbsp coconut aminos** (Simple Truth Organic: 500 mg sodium/Tbsp, no alcohol, soy-free, free from cereals). Tamari was dropped because tamari/soy sauce lists alcohol as an ingredient
- Skip the brown sugar (coconut aminos is already sweet)
- 1 Tbsp unseasoned rice vinegar (pantry; skip if you have none)
- 2 Tbsp honey
- 1 Tbsp brown sugar
- **1½ Tbsp** ginger paste
- ¼ tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- ½ cup unsalted GF broth

**Label:** *RAW BEEF — pressure-cook to 145°F + 3-min rest. Thaw in the fridge, cook within 1–2 days. Cooking day: add ½ cup water (meet your cooker's minimum liquid) + 3 cups frozen broccoli.*
**Cooking day:**
1. Add ½ cup water (check that the total liquid meets the Midea manual's minimum). Pressure-cook on high 8 min, then quick release. Thermometer: **145°F**, then rest 3 min.
2. Switch to sauté and stir in 1 Tbsp cornstarch mixed with 2 Tbsp water.
3. Add 3 cups broccoli, cover (not pressurized) and cook 3–4 min.
4. Serve with optional rice from the rice cooker.

## 5. Chicken, Mushroom & Green Bean — RAW sheet-pan bags (2)
**Per bag:**
- 1¼ lb boneless thighs, in 1½-inch pieces
- 8 oz mushrooms
- 12 oz green beans, trimmed
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 1½ tsp Italian seasoning (salt-free)
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ¼ tsp garlic powder
- **¼ tsp salt**
- ¼ tsp pepper

**Label:** *RAW CHICKEN — cook to 165°F. Thaw in the fridge, cook within 1–2 days.*
**Cooking day:** roast on **2 lined sheet pans** (one pan crowds it and steams) at 425°F for 22–25 min, or air-fry at 400°F for about 15 min, shaking halfway. Chicken must reach **165°F**. The green beans will be softer than fresh (they were frozen raw).

## Freezing and reheating quality notes
- **Tetrazzini:** GF pasta softens in the freezer, so cook it 2–3 min under package time. The sauce may look slightly grainy after thawing; stir halfway through baking.
- **Enchiladas:** corn tortillas soften. Bake uncovered for the last 10 min.
- **Tater Tot:** best crisped uncovered. You can also re-crisp a portion in the air fryer.
- **Raw bags:** freeze well. Mushrooms and green beans soften slightly.
- **Storage:** best quality within about 3 months. Food that stays frozen at 0°F remains safe beyond that.
