## When it's done (about 15 minutes hands-on)
You have 2 hours to get everything in the fridge. Aim for much sooner.
1. Write down the time it came out. Turn the oven off.
2. Lift the meat out and pull it into large chunks in a shallow container.
3. Strain the liquid into shallow containers, 2 inches deep or less. Press the vegetables for their juice, then discard them.
4. Refrigerate everything loosely covered. Cover it fully once cold. **Don't put the hot Dutch oven in the fridge.**
5. Leave it overnight. Tell Jay it's done to get the steps for removing the fat, reducing the sauce, portioning and labeling.
